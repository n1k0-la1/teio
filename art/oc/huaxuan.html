The young master and heir of a trading company and tea pavilion in a religious tourist town. His name isn't actually Cale, Giamin just started calling him that and it's caught on as a nickname.

Has a weird complex with his looks, since everyone says he's a splitting image of his mother, but he's never seen her face. She died giving birth to him, and his father's taken down all her pictures. Often crossdresses in her old clothes, hugging himself to pretend she's there. Hates himself after every time because he feels like he's 'getting rid of the traces of her'. Is friends with some of the working girls at the bigger brothels in town since they teach him how to do makeup. They're the only ones who know about his 'hobby' because he's terrified of it getting out, even though nobody'd probably care.

Strong sense of self loathing, but never self-mutilates since it feels like he's once again ruining his mother's body. He has too many people relying on him to kick the bucket anyways. Took up smoking madak when his grandmother died, and seemingly doesn't realize it's another form of self harm for him.

Doesn't believe in the gods, not anymore. He knows they exist, of course, it's hard to dispute that. But ever since his grandmother—the only person who was there for him as a child—died, ever since a glimmer of hope was dangled in front of him and so violently ripped away, he hasn't prayed once.

🡆 About Kemi-Sama 🡆 About Giamin

If prompted, he'll say he 'isn't particularly religious', but in truth, he hates the god. When he was very young, his grandmother grew deathly ill. The end comes for us all, but to a child, this notion was world shattering. How could the only person who takes care of him die? -This had to be a mistake, right? Day and night he'd pray, beg Kemi-sama to do something. Sometimes even sneaking out of the estate to pray at the temple in the dead of night. It was fruitless for a long time, and he was about to give up when- He saw that kitsune in the woods. His grandmother always told him that although the creature's mischievous ways could cause lots of trouble for them; they were wise beings, with power a human like him could only dream of. Was this- Was this it? Did Kemi-sama finally notice his desperate prayers and send him a being that could invoke a miracle? In his excitement, he rushed over to the white-haired being, only for it to startle and run away. The boy tried desperately to run after it- Apologize- Explain himself- Beg for forgiveness-Anything! But his short, child legs could not catch up in the rough terrain. There was nothing he could do. He does not believe in god. -For no being of mercy would dangle hope so closely only to rip it away.

Long long ago, when Cale was still a child with whimsy and hope; he saw what he thought was a Kitsune in the woods. He chased after the tall-eared being, hoping for a wish to heal his grandmother. Unluckily for him, he was unable to catch up with it; only left with a broken-off half of a tassel to prove that the being existed at all. Little did either of them know, that the so-called 'Kitsune' was Giamin. In a passionate fever only possible by one who has the energy of a child; Little-Cale threw himself into every book he could get his hands on about Kitsune, trying to find out more about that ever-so-elusive beautiful figure he saw that day; Hoping that they would meet again. By the time the two finally did meet; they were unrecognizable to the other. Cale had long grown up, and being a beast-man; Giamin had stayed the same. The now grown-up heir refused to believe the two were one-and-the-same, refused to even consider the possibility. But... Even he can concede that their striking white hair is awfully similar... Maybe he'll let him stay around for a little longer. Just to gaze into that mirror of the past again.
